Design and planning documents for the plugins-kit project: implementation plans, migration plans, design proposals, decision records, and status documents for its subsystems.

This is where plugins-kit keeps its design and planning material: the documents that work out a subsystem's shape, sequence its build, record where a change landed, and hold the evidence behind a decision. It is organized one directory per subsystem, and inside each the files split by job: a design record, an implementation or migration plan, a status or milestone list, or supporting evidence. Nothing here ships into a plugin cache; it is maintainer-facing and points at the shipped documentation and source it describes.

Where to start

Open docs/planning/plugin-improvements.md first: it is the cross-subsystem staging list of open deficiencies, each with its problem and a candidate fix, and it marks the resolved ones. For a specific subsystem, go straight to that subsystem's directory.
