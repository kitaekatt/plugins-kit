Planning documents that design, measure, and validate md-domain CLAUDE.md coverage and generation.

These are the planning, measurement, and validation documents for the md-domain coverage lane: the opt-in feature that audits whether a code subtree's hazards are actually carried by the agent instruction files that load for it. The lane shipped in skills-kit 0.62.0, so much of the folder now reads as an after-action record rather than a live design. The documents fall into three roles, and that order is the folder's shape: the design of the lane, the measurements from running it on real corpora, and the outside verdicts on what it produced. Each role feeds the next, and the goal the lane was built to prove (that its documents change what a code review finds) is the one the evidence does not yet settle. Nothing here ships to consumers; the shipped summary lives in the md-domain references.

Where to start

Start with the docs/planning/md-domain-coverage-lane/README.md: it states the scope that was once misread (this verb is not a code-review tool), the shipped status, and what to read before building. It is the one document that maps the folder to the shipped summary, so it is the natural re-entry point after time away.

Routes

  • Trace a false claim to its root cause
    1. docs/planning/md-domain-coverage-lane/sol-adjudication-2026-08-13.md fixes the keep/revert call and names the ten false claims
    2. docs/planning/md-domain-coverage-lane/claim-verification-forensics-2026-08-13.md then reads the recovered transcripts to show why the lane's own checks passed them
  • Read the regeneration measurement in order
    1. docs/planning/md-domain-coverage-lane/regeneration-run-2026-08-12.md is the first completed run; it states its confound (pre-amendment reports), so its loss figure is an upper bound
    2. docs/planning/md-domain-coverage-lane/regeneration-run-2026-08-12-fresh-reports.md supersedes it with fresh reports and closes the confound, measuring delivery loss at 1.6%
  • Design then result of the review experiment
    1. docs/planning/md-domain-coverage-lane/review-effect-experiment.md is the pre-registered two-arm design, fixed before any reviewer was dispatched
    2. docs/planning/md-domain-coverage-lane/review-effect-results.md reports the null result and the two findings behind it

The design of the lane

  • docs/planning/md-domain-coverage-lane/coverage-lane-spec.md -- the settled spec for the opt-in, report-only coverage verb: the subject, intent gating, exclusions, and the GAPS-FOUND and COVERAGE-ASSESSED verdicts. Open this to know what the lane does and what it refuses.
  • docs/planning/md-domain-coverage-lane/hierarchy-analysis-design.md -- a proposal, not yet implemented, for a tree-scale resolution lane that gives every written or proposed fact one home across a whole tree.
  • docs/planning/md-domain-coverage-lane/regression-test-design.md -- the design of the pre-registered recall test: a fixed answer key, per-mechanism scoring, and the negative controls that stop a flag-everything result passing.

Measuring the lane

  • docs/planning/md-domain-coverage-lane/generation-deficiencies-and-plan.md -- measured candidate losses across a real corpus, with its retractions, and the remediation plan that has not been implemented. The G-numbers and the P0 decision are here.
  • docs/planning/md-domain-coverage-lane/regeneration-run-2026-08-12.md -- the first completed end-to-end run ( 0.52.0) and its stated confound: it was fed pre-amendment reports, so its loss figure is an upper bound.
  • docs/planning/md-domain-coverage-lane/regeneration-run-2026-08-12-fresh-reports.md -- the 0.53.0 fresh-reports run that supersedes the one above and closes the confound; delivery loss in the designed configuration came in at 1.6%.
  • docs/planning/md-domain-coverage-lane/regeneration-ledger.md -- the audit findings split into workflow flaws (fix, regenerate, re-test) and held observations (record, and do not act until sign-off).
  • docs/planning/md-domain-coverage-lane/defect-history-p-series.md -- the P-series of corpus-scale defects, each with the proposal it produced. Read its Known limit before quoting any figure.

Verdicts on its output

  • docs/planning/md-domain-coverage-lane/sol-adjudication-2026-08-13.md -- the independent keep/revert call over 13 generated documents (3 keep, 10 revert), acted on rather than weighed.
  • docs/planning/md-domain-coverage-lane/claim-verification-forensics-2026-08-13.md -- companion to the adjudication: from recovered transcripts, why the ten false claims passed the lane's own checks, and the acceptance corpus any fix must meet.
  • docs/planning/md-domain-coverage-lane/review-effect-experiment.md -- the pre-registered documentation-on/off review experiment: does a generated document change what a blind review finds?
  • docs/planning/md-domain-coverage-lane/review-effect-results.md -- that experiment's result: no measurable effect, plus the two findings (the carried facts were cheap to reach, and one was untrue as restated).
  • docs/planning/md-domain-coverage-lane/parked-test-cpp-corpus.md -- the parked C++ removal test and everything needed to resume it without re-deriving the design; it is the task's next action.
