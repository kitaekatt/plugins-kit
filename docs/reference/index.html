plugins-kit maintainer reference: standards, design records, procedures, platform facts, and dated measurements

    This is the maintainer reference for plugins-kit. It holds the standards that bind plugin authors, signed-off architecture records, operational procedures for publishing and releasing, static platform facts, and dated measurements documenting what was tried and why it did or did not work. The organizing principle: every document here is a decision record or a standing rule, not a tutorial. Open one when you need to justify a change against a criterion, or to recover why a design settled where it did.

    Where to start

    If you are about to change a plugin's shipped behavior, open docs/reference/plugin-opinion-razor.md first. It is the gate every config-seam and publish decision passes through, and its criteria are the most cited in review.
